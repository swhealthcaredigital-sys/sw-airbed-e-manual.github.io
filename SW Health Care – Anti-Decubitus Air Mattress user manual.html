Built to take pressure off bony areas

Constant pressure on a bony spot is the leading cause of skin breakdown. The continuous movement of this mattress lessens that pressure and improves circulation.

Alternating pressure therapy

The pump fills cells A and B in turns. Each cell inflates and deflates on a timer (about 12 minutes for a complete cycle), so no single spot carries the body’s weight for long.

Set up in six steps

What’s in the box: the air mattress, pump with control panel, tubing and a repair kit.

  1. Place the mattress

    Lay it on the bed frame with the hose end at the foot section of the bed.

  2. Mount the pump

    Hang the pump securely on the bed’s foot end with the integrated hooks, or set it on a smooth, flat surface.

  3. Connect the hoses

    Attach the air hoses from the mattress to the pump.

  4. Plug in

    Connect to a wall outlet and route the power cord safely away from possible hazards.

  5. Switch on

    Turn on the power switch on the pump’s control panel. The mattress starts to inflate.

  6. Adjust the pressure

    Once inflated, set the dial on the pump to the patient’s comfort.

Cautions

  • Do not smoke on or near the pump.
  • Keep the pump away from heated surfaces.
  • Power input: ~220 V, 50 Hz, 0.1 A.
